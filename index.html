01The problem with calculators

Most retirement tools draw one straight line. Retirement isn't straight.

74%

The spread in projected retirement income when a 2024 review fed the same household into 22 superannuation fund calculators. Same person. Wildly different futures. A straight-line average with a bell curve stapled on is not a plan; it's a guess wearing a suit.

02From history to the future · scroll
STAGE 1 / 5 — INGEST

Start with everything that ever happened.

Feed it every month of history you have: returns for every asset class, plus inflation, per market. The more it's given, the more booms, busts and shocks it learns from. Nothing smoothed, nothing thrown away.

monthly total returns · CPI · per-jurisdiction currency basis · provenance-hashed source files
STAGE 2 / 5 — DECOMPOSE

Pull the signal apart from the noise.

CEEMDAN, an adaptive signal-processing method borrowed from engineering, splits each series into its oscillating components: long trend, business cycles and pure noise. We keep the structure and model the randomness separately.

CEEMDAN · 50 noise-assisted realisations · up to 6 intrinsic mode functions · ARIMA artefact diagnostics
STAGE 3 / 5 — RESPECT THE TAILS

Crashes are not 'six-sigma events'.

Real markets fall harder and more often than a bell curve allows. Extreme-value theory fits the tails directly, so 1987, 2008 and 2020-style shocks show up in the simulations as often as history says they should.

generalised Pareto tail fit · EVT on the extreme decile · no normality assumption
STAGE 4 / 5 — DETECT REGIMES

Calm markets and stressed markets behave differently.

The engine learns when history was in a calm state and when it was under stress, then lets simulated futures switch between them. Bad years cluster, just like they do in real life.

regime switching · rolling 12-month volatility · stress threshold at 75th percentile
STAGE 5 / 5 — RESAMPLE THE FUTURE

Rebuild thousands of futures, block by block.

Whole blocks of real history are stitched together into new, plausible futures, preserving how markets actually move from month to month. Then every household's tax, super, pension and spending rules run inside every path, every month.

stationary block bootstrap · seeded, reproducible RNG · monthly tax/pension ledger per path
